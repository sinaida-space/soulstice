# Making from inside your own time

Method notes for layer 9. This layer fails more often than any other, and it fails in a
predictable way: the user produces a competent essay about technology and alienation
that any of ten thousand people could have written. That essay is layer 4 material
wearing layer 9 clothes.

The aim is not commentary on the present. The aim is to locate what the present has
made possible to *feel*, and to find where this particular person is out of step with it.

---

## Four positions worth holding

**Agamben: the contemporary is out of joint.** Being contemporary is not coinciding
with your time. It is a relationship of disconnection and anachronism: those who fit
their era too comfortably cannot see it, because they are inside its light. The
contemporary perceives the darkness of the present rather than its illumination.

*Operational form:* ask what they refuse. An artist entirely at ease with their moment
has nothing to say about it. The refusal question in layer 9 is not a rhetorical
flourish; it is the layer's main instrument.

**Raymond Williams: structure of feeling.** Williams offered this against zeitgeist,
which he thought idealist and vague. A structure of feeling is the lived, felt texture
of a present moment: meanings and values as actively experienced, before they have
hardened into institutions or ideology. It is emergent, and art is where it becomes
articulable before anywhere else.

*Operational form:* ask for the felt quality, not the idea. The useful question is
"what is possible to feel now that was not possible to feel twenty years ago", and the
answer must name a feeling. A device does not count. Feelings that have no name yet
are the target.

**Bourdieu: position in a field.** Nobody makes from nowhere. What looks like personal
taste is substantially a position in a field of possibilities, shaped by an acquired
disposition and by what is available to be done. Originality is often a structural
position rather than an inner property.

*Operational form:* "what does your field currently reward, and what does it currently
make unthinkable?" Whatever is unthinkable in a field is where the room is. This
connects back to layer 2, where the disposition arrived early.

**Terry Smith: contemporaneity, not modernity.** The present is not one arrow of time
but many incompatible presents running simultaneously, without a single direction of
progress. There is no longer a next thing that everyone is heading toward.

*Operational form:* this frees the user from the obligation to be ahead. "Which present
are you making in?" is a better question than "is this current?" There is no single
current to be behind.

---

## Question sequence for layer 9

Run in order. Do not skip to the refusal question.

1. "What is possible to feel now that was not possible to feel twenty years ago? Not a
   technology. A feeling."
2. "What does everyone you know understand without explanation, that would take an hour
   to explain to someone from 1995?"
3. "What is your generation numb to? What is it ashamed of?"
4. "Name something this time makes easy that ought to be hard. And something it makes
   hard that ought to be easy."
5. "What is being lost right now that nobody is documenting?"
6. "Where are you deliberately out of step? What do you refuse to be current about?"
7. "Your tools belong to this moment and they have preferences. What do your tools want
   you to make? What happens if you make the thing they resist?"

---

## Failure modes

**The think-piece.** Fluent, general, correct, borrowed. Diagnose by asking for a
first-person example from the last month. If none exists, the position is not theirs.

**Nostalgia.** "Everything is worse now" is an inherited posture, layer 2 material.
Redirect: "what is genuinely better now that you find it hard to admit is better?"

**Panic.** Apocalyptic framing feels like insight and functions as anaesthetic; it
excuses making nothing. Redirect to a specific afternoon.

**Chasing.** Making work about whatever is currently discussed. Also not layer 9.
That is layer 4. Note it as such and move it there.

**Refusal as pose.** Being against everything current is as unfree as riding it. Test:
"what specifically are you protecting by staying out of that?" A refusal with something
concrete behind it is real; one without is a costume.

---

## What a good answer looks like

Two sentences, both in first person, both slightly awkward:

- one felt quality of the present that has no established name yet, arrived at from
  their own experience rather than from commentary;
- one refusal, with a stated thing it protects.

Together these are the position from which the work is made. In the Compass, quote them
exactly. Do not smooth the awkwardness. The awkwardness is the part that is theirs.
