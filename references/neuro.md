# Why the moves work

Practical neuropsychology behind the instrument. This exists so you make good decisions
about pacing, wording and when to stop. **Never explain any of this to the user in
session.** Telling someone their default mode network is constructing a self-narrative
turns an inquiry into a lecture and stops the inquiry.

---

## The self is a narrative that gets rebuilt on retrieval

Autobiographical memory is reconstructive, not archival. The default mode network
(medial prefrontal cortex, posterior cingulate, angular gyrus) assembles a personal
narrative on demand and preferentially retrieves material that fits the current
self-model. It works for coherence, and accuracy is incidental to it.

**Consequences for this instrument:**

- Asking "who are you as an artist" gets a well-rehearsed narrative reassembled from
  the same coherent material as last time. That answer is nearly useless.
- Asking for a specific episode ("the last time it went well: what time of day, what
  was in the room") bypasses the summary and pulls episodic detail that has not been
  pre-filtered for coherence.
- **This is why the concreteness ladder is the core technique.** It is not a stylistic
  preference. Abstract self-description queries the narrative; episodic recall queries
  the material the narrative was built from, including the parts it discarded.
- Retrieved memories are labile for a window afterward and are re-stored in their new
  context. This is why the sequence matters: recalling an old shame *after* Arc I has
  reframed it changes what gets re-stored. It is also why you must not push into real
  trauma. The same lability that makes reframing possible makes careless handling
  harmful.

## Recognition is cheap, correction is not

Recognising a fitting description is far easier than generating one. That is the
risk this skill runs by using choice cards everywhere: someone can pick four plausible
options in a row and never once say anything of her own.

It is also the opportunity. Producing a self-description from nothing is a hard
generative task performed under evaluation, and it returns the cached narrative.
Judging whether a specific proposed sentence is true of you is a comparison against
actual episodic memory, and it returns a verdict with a reason attached.

**Consequences:**

- The card is not the question. The card is the stimulus; the question is "what is
  wrong with this". Never let a pick stand unattacked.
- Options that are all slightly wrong outperform options that include the right
  answer. A near-miss forces her to locate the difference, and the difference is the
  material. Build layer 5 and layer 9 this way deliberately.
- A card whose options are generic returns nothing, because a generic option matches
  everyone and therefore produces no comparison against memory. This is the whole
  reason `references/cards.md` bans category options.
- Watch for autopilot: three fast picks with no "Other" text and no hesitation means
  the options are too comfortable. Raise the cost of the next card.

## Naming a feeling reduces its grip

Affect labelling, putting a specific word to an emotional state, is associated with
reduced amygdala response and increased right ventrolateral prefrontal activity.
Notably it is not the same as self-reflection; the effect comes from the labelling
itself.

**Consequences:**

- "What is the worry?" is a working intervention, not merely data collection. Layer 1
  and layer 7 do actual work simply by getting things named precisely.
- Precision matters more than intensity. "Fear of being seen as decorative by people
  whose technical opinion I respect" does more than "fear of judgement".
- Do not label *for* them. A label you supply does not produce the effect and does
  produce compliance. A card option is a supplied label, so the labelling has to
  happen in the correction beat: she picks the near-miss, then says in her own words
  what it actually is. That sentence is the one that does the work.

## Prediction error is the condition for updating

The brain is a prediction machine; it updates a model only when a prediction is
violated. An inquiry that confirms what the person already believes about themselves
changes nothing, however pleasant it feels.

**Consequences:**

- Contradiction is the productive event. Hold both halves and do not tidy them.
- Kegan & Lahey's assumption test works because it engineers a prediction error in the
  world rather than in conversation. Belief revision from argument is weak; belief
  revision from a violated prediction in real experience is strong. Always convert an
  insight into one small testable action.
- If a session produces only agreement and recognition, it has failed. Provoke.

## Interoception carries the aesthetic judgement

Anterior insula tracks internal bodily state, and interoceptive signals participate in
evaluation and decision-making well before verbal justification arrives. Artists
routinely have a reliable somatic "this is wrong" that precedes any argument for why.

**Consequences:**

- Layer 8 asks for the physical signal deliberately. "Where do you feel it when the
  work is right" is a real question with a real answer, and the answer is portable:
  they can use it tomorrow.
- Verbal reasons for aesthetic decisions are largely post-hoc confabulation. Trust the
  bodily marker they report over the theory they attach to it, and say so if the two
  conflict.

---

## Seeking is not liking

The dopaminergic system drives pursuit and anticipation; a separate system handles
consummatory pleasure. Wanting and enjoying come apart, and the gap widens under
variable-ratio reward, which is precisely what a social feed, an open-call cycle, or
a follower count is.

**Consequences:**

- Layer 4 exists because of this. An artist can want something intensely and get
  nothing from having it. The ten-year question ("would you still make it if nobody
  saw the result for ten years") separates the two systems cleanly.
- Envy in layer 7 is a wanting signal with poor aim. It reliably points at something
  unclaimed; it is unreliable about what.

---

## Cognitive load degrades honest answers

Working memory is small and self-referential processing is expensive. Fatigue pushes
people toward cached self-descriptions, which is exactly the failure mode this
instrument is trying to avoid.

**Consequences:**

- One question per card. Never batch two questions into one card, and never fire two
  cards before reading the first answer.
- Cards reduce load compared to open questions, which is why this skill can run ten
  layers where an open-question version would exhaust someone by layer 6. Spend the
  saved capacity on the correction beats, not on more layers.
- The arc breaks are load management, not ceremony. Take them.
- A long passage is better across three sittings than one. Say this out loud when
  offering the mode.
- Late-session answers are lower quality. Do not schedule the hardest layers last,
  which is why Field, the most important layer, sits at position 5.

---

## Incubation and diffuse attention

Insight is associated with reduced external attention and increased default-mode
activity just before the solution arrives. Sustained forcing suppresses it.

**Consequences:**

- After a hard question, do not fill the space with a second question or a summary.
- Recommend that the Compass be read once and left for a week before acting on it.
- The journal mode exists for this reason: spacing outperforms a single long session
  for anything meant to change behaviour.

---

## Threat closes the aperture

Under social-evaluative threat, cognition narrows to defence and self-justification.
An inquiry that feels like an exam produces performance, not material.

**Consequences:**

- Never evaluate an answer. No "good", no "interesting", no "that's very honest".
  Praise signals that answers are being graded, and grading produces performances.
- Provoke the material, never the person. "That sounds like an artist statement" is
  a comment on a sentence. "You're being evasive" is a comment on them, and will cost
  you the rest of the session.
- If defensiveness appears, drop to a concrete episodic question. Detail is safer than
  self-assessment, and it goes deeper anyway.
