# Positions on finding yourself

Twelve philosophical positions on selfhood, each converted into a question that can
actually be asked. Method notes for the collapses the layers keep running into.

## The rule: none of this is ever spoken

**The philosophy stays hidden, exactly as the psycho-esoteric vocabulary in
`references/layers.md` stays hidden.** No philosopher’s name is said in session, ever.
Not Sartre, not Nietzsche, not the Stoics, not Zhuangzi. You never say “there is a
concept called bad faith”, you never say “as Camus put it”, you never offer a
quotation to the user, you never name a school or a tradition. No card label, no
description, no header, no line of the Compass carries a name.

The position works invisibly: it decides which question gets asked and how the options
are shaped. The person hears a plain sentence about her own life and has no idea it
came from anywhere. Naming a source has a specific cost: once a position has an
author, she can agree with the author instead of answering, and agreement is worthless
here. Only her own sentence is worth anything. This file exists so the structure is
auditable by whoever reads it, like the origin note at the end of `layers.md`. At most
one short phrase per thinker appears in quotation marks below, and it appears **only
in this file**. Nothing quoted here is ever copied into a card.

There is no philosophy layer and these do not run in order. Each is held in reserve
for one collapse: she says the sentence, you reach for the matching position. Read
`references/cards.md` first. Every question below is an `AskUserQuestion` card, and
the options are English examples for an artist working with projection and moving
bodies. Rebuild them out of her own words. Text after each middle dot is the option’s
`description` field.

## The positions

### 1. Freedom and self-deception

A person has no fixed nature. She is what she makes of what was made of her, and she
is answerable for that even while experiencing herself as determined. The standard
evasion converts a chosen ongoing behaviour into a fixed property, so that a decision
stops requiring deciding. The name for it is “bad faith”.

**Answers:** “I had no choice”, “this is just who I am”, “circumstances made me”.
**Operational form:** what are you calling fixed that you choose again every week?

> **Q:** One thing you say about yourself as if it were a fact. Which do you say most
> often? · **header:** Fixed

- I am not good at asking people for things · never tested against a named person
- I am technical, the concept side is someone else’s job · a deal you made once
- I do not have the temperament for a big project · one you were never given
- I am incapable of finishing anything · said about a year in which you finished four

Beat two: “When did you decide that, and what did deciding it save you from?”
**Failure mode:** it becomes accusation. “You are choosing this” lands as blame and
ends the session. Keep the card descriptive and let her draw the conclusion. Real
constraints exist; the card asks only which named one has been tested recently.

### 2. Thrownness

Nobody selects their starting conditions: country, family, first language, year, body,
whichever training happened to be available. You always begin already underway, in a
situation you did not author. What can be chosen is the stance taken toward it, and
whether the inherited possibilities get taken up on purpose or run on unexamined.

**Answers:** “I did not choose where I started”, “I began too late”, “the people I
compare myself to started at nineteen with money”.
**Operational form:** which parts of the starting position have you since taken up
deliberately?

> **Q:** Things about your starting position you did not choose. Which one have you
> since made into a working method? · **header:** Start

- Coming to this from an engineering job · the habit of systems is now the material
- Arriving where you knew nobody · every relationship was chosen rather than given
- Being self-taught in the tools · nothing in the workflow is standard, mistakes included
- None of it, it is all still a handicap · keep this if it is the true answer

**Failure mode:** mysticism. This one inflates into destiny, authenticity and being
faster than anything else here. Every option must be a fact with a date on it; if an
option could go on a poster, delete it. Ask about the country, the year, the money and
the training, and keep the vocabulary of authenticity out entirely.

### 3. Affirming what happened

A life is not improved by wishing its history away, because that history produced the
person doing the wishing. The strong move affirms the whole sequence, wasted years
included, as the price of the present position rather than a debt against it. A self
gets built through repeated overcoming. “Amor fati” names the willingness.

**Answers:** “I regret the years I lost”, “I would be further along if I had started
earlier”, “those eight years were waste”.
**Operational form:** what would you have to be willing to affirm, the lost years
included, for your present position to be yours?

> **Q:** Suppose the lost years are the reason the work is what it is. Which part
> would you have to be willing to claim? · **header:** Claim

- The corporate years taught me to run a project to a deadline · a rare skill in your field
- The failed pieces taught me what I cannot stand · taste arrived at by elimination
- The late start is why I am not competing with people my age · a different clock
- Nothing, they were waste and calling them useful is a lie · do not argue this away

Beat two: “What would you have made at twenty-two that you are glad does not exist?”
**Failure mode:** forced positivity. This becomes a gratitude exercise within two
cards, and a gratitude exercise produces nothing. The fourth option must always be
available, and if she takes it, write it down and move.

### 4. The absurd

A person keeps demanding sense from a world that returns no answer. The gap between
the demand and the silence is the absurd, and it does not close. The move refuses both
suicide of the body and suicide of the intellect, which is the leap into a borrowed
meaning, and continues with the question left open. The continuing is the answer.

**Answers:** “why bother”, “nobody needs it”, “it changes nothing”, “the world is on
fire and I am making light on a wall”.
**Operational form:** the question has no answer. What do you do on the Tuesday after
admitting that? **This card does not resolve the question and must not try.**

> **Q:** Suppose none of it is needed and nothing changes because of it. The next
> morning, what do you actually do? · **header:** Tuesday

- Open the same file and keep working, question left open · continuing without a reason
- Stop for a month and find out whether I come back · a real experiment, real result
- Find a reason that holds, even a borrowed one · relief bought with a story
- Keep working and stay angry about it · anger as fuel, which lasts about two years

**This is the single most useful position in Ground, the crisis mode.** In collapse no
reason offered will hold, because the machinery that accepts reasons has stopped, and
offering one is experienced as not being heard. This position works with the collapse
instead of arguing against it. Reach for it first there; the others come later.

**Failure mode:** consolation. The instinct is to answer the question. Do not. A card
that supplies meaning here is one she accepts politely and leaves unchanged.

### 5. Meaning under conditions you did not choose

Meaning is not a possession to be lost or a mood to be induced. It is found in a
specific task, in a specific relation to another person, or in the stance taken toward
unavoidable suffering. It arrives situation by situation, and the person is answerable
to it rather than the reverse.

**Answers:** “there is no point”, “nobody is waiting for this”, “no one would notice
if I stopped”.
**Operational form:** meaning found in doing the work and meaning granted by an
audience are two different things. Which one has actually gone missing?

> **Q:** When you say it has no point, which point is missing? · **header:** Point

- Nobody is asking for it, making it is like talking to a wall · the audience withdrew
- The hours have gone flat even when nobody is watching · the work withdrew, the serious one
- I can do it, I cannot say why it should be me · a question about position
- One person stopped answering and it went quiet · a named person, which is workable

That distinction is the whole value here. Meaning in the work is recoverable by
working. Meaning granted by an audience was never under her control, and once it is
separated out, position 8 takes over.

**Failure mode:** a lecture about purpose. One card, one distinction, then hand the
material to whichever position her answer selects.

### 6. The story a life makes

A life is intelligible as a narrative, and a practice makes sense only inside a story
with a beginning, a middle and something being pursued. Where nobody can say what
story they are in, individual acts stay disconnected and cannot be assessed. “What am
I to do” depends on the prior question of what story one is part of.

**Answers:** “my work is scattered”, “there is no through-line”, “the projects have
nothing to do with each other”.
**Operational form:** what story makes the scattered things one life? Options come
from her own works list in layer 5.

> **Q:** Your list read as one story rather than as separate jobs. Which of these
> stories fits worst? · **header:** Story

- Someone putting a body inside a machine image · twelve years, one problem, four disguises
- Someone taking an engineering habit where it was unwanted · the method is the line
- Someone who took whatever paid and got good at everything · honest, and still a story
- Someone leaving a profession slowly enough to survive it · the transition itself

**Failure mode:** retrospective fiction. This position manufactures a clean origin myth
faster than anything else on the list, and a clean myth is unusable. Three preventions,
all mandatory. Ask which story fits **worst**, as above. Require the story to account
for one piece she is ashamed of. Then run one card on what the chosen story cannot
explain, and put that in the Compass beside the story. A through-line that explains
everything explains nothing.

### 7. The background a choice is made against

The demand to be yourself collapses if the self is chosen against nothing. A choice
matters only against a horizon of things that already matter independently of the
choosing: a tradition, a community, a history, a demand made by the world. Remove the
background and every option weighs the same, which is why pure self-expression turns
hollow. Significance is never conferred by preferring.

**Answers:** “I just want to do my own thing”, “I do not want to be part of any
scene”, “I only answer to myself”.
**Operational form:** your own thing against what? Which background makes the choices
count?

> **Q:** If the background your work stands against disappeared tomorrow, which loss
> would empty the work out? · **header:** Ground

- Live performance and everyone who stood in a dark room · without it, wallpaper
- The people who taught you, the ones you argue with · nothing left to argue with
- A city and what is currently possible in it · without it the work has no address
- Nothing would change, it stands on its own · beat two asks who it is for

**Failure mode:** it sounds like a demand to join something. It is a question about
what she is already answerable to, which is more than she will admit. Never propose a
background. Make her name it.

### 8. What is up to you

Some things are within your power and some are not. Judgement, effort, choice of work
and how you respond are yours. Reputation, invitations, funding, whether anyone shows
up, what the field rewards this decade are not. Distress comes from placing hope in
the second column; correct placement returns full effort to the first and drops the
second from the accounting.

**Answers:** “I cannot control whether they invite me”, “forty applications and
nothing back”, “the same names get everything”.
**Operational form:** sort what is actually yours, then act at full strength inside it.

> **Q:** The last rejection. Which part of it was inside your control? ·
> **header:** Control

- The material was not ready and I sent it anyway · yours, correctable this month
- The application was fine and they wanted something else · theirs, nothing to correct
- I applied to the wrong call because it was open · a choice of target, still yours
- I did not apply to the one I actually wanted · yours, and the costliest

**Distinguish this sharply from resignation.** Resignation says it is out of my hands,
so I will do less. This position says the reverse: because reception is out of your
hands there is nothing left to protect, so the work goes out at full strength and the
counting of invitations stops. If a card in this family produces less effort, it was
built wrong. Test with a beat two: “What would you send this month if the answer were
already decided?”

### 9. The useless tree

Effort pushed against the grain of a situation produces friction and exhaustion.
Acting with the grain, unforced, produces more with less. And there is a tree so
gnarled and useless that no carpenter ever cut it down, which is exactly why it grew
old and enormous. Being of no use is a condition of survival rather than a verdict.

**Answers:** “I am forcing everything”, “my work has to justify itself”, “nobody needs
it”.
**Operational form:** what would you make if it were not required to be of use to
anyone?

> **Q:** Everything you make has to earn its place. What would you make if it were
> allowed to be of no use at all? · **header:** Useless

- The thing with no audience, no venue, no explanation · it survives by being unwanted
- The one I call a study so it never has to be finished · already useless, disguised
- Nothing, if it has no use I have no reason to start · this names the actual engine
- Everything I make is already useless and that is the problem · the inversion

“Nobody needs it” is treated here as the good news. A thing nobody needs is a thing
nobody can take, redirect, or commission out of shape. Do not explain that to her.
Build the card so it is felt.

**Failure mode:** it licenses inertia. Wu wei is never passivity and this must never
become permission to stop. Pair it with a card on what she is forcing specifically, so
the finding redirects effort rather than removing it.

### 10. No fixed self

There is no permanent unchanging self underneath experience. What is there is a stream
of processes: perceptions, habits, intentions, all changing, none of them a core. The
suffering in “I am a fraud” comes from defending a fixed thing that is not there to be
defended. A person is a practice rather than an essence.

**Answers:** “I am a fraud”, “I am not really an artist”, “they will find out”.
**Operational form:** the claim is about a fixed thing. Ask what would have to be
true, in acts, for the sentence to be false.

> **Q:** “I am not really an artist.” What would have to be true for that sentence to
> be false? · **header:** Really

- An institution I respect would say so in writing · a verdict held by someone else
- I would have to make a living from it · a money test standing in for an identity test
- One person I respect would take one work seriously · it may have happened already
- It cannot be false, it is a fact about me · the fixed thing, stated plainly

If she takes the last option, beat two: “When was it last false? Name the day.” One
counter-example dissolves a permanent claim into an episodic one.

**Handle carefully.** This is never a denial of her experience. “There is no self, so
your feeling is unreal” is cruel and false to the position itself. The feeling is
real; the claim is what gets examined, with dates and events. Never use the vocabulary
of emptiness, no-self or illusion. Ask about acts.

### 11. Despair as a relation to yourself

Despair is a relation gone wrong with itself: wanting to be somebody other than the
person you are, or refusing to be anybody in particular. It is often quiet and fully
compatible with a functioning life, which is why it goes unnoticed. The commonest form
is a settled refusal of the self one actually is.

**Answers:** “I want to be a different kind of artist than I am”, “I am fine, I just
do not want to be me doing this”.
**Operational form:** who is the artist you keep wishing you were, and what does
wishing it cost you every week?

> **Q:** The artist you keep wishing you were. Which one? · **header:** Wish

- One I know personally, whose year has gone better than mine · a named person
- The one I would have become with a different training · a fork you rehearse often
- Myself five years ago, before I knew this much · the version with less to lose

**Failure mode:** it approaches clinical ground. If the answers point somewhere darker
than a comparison habit, that is Ground material: follow `references/crisis.md`.

### 12. Attention

Attention held on something outside yourself, without trying to seize it, is the
rarest and most generous capacity a person has. It requires suspending your own agenda
long enough for the thing to be seen as it is. Most of what passes for looking is the
imagination filling in what it expected. Real attention is close to a form of waiting.

**Answers:** “everything I make is about me and I am sick of it”, “I have run out of
material”.
**Operational form:** what has your attention when the work goes well, and when did
you last look at something without needing it for anything?

> **Q:** When the work goes well, what actually has your attention? ·
> **header:** Looking

- The material doing something I did not predict · attention on the thing itself
- How it will read to the people who see it · attention on the room
- My own reflection in it, and whether it is impressive · honest, and the tiring one
- Nothing in particular, the hours just pass · pairs with layer 8, the somatic markers

**Failure mode:** aesthetic mysticism. Keep it to one remembered afternoon and one
named object.

## Where each gets used

These attach to existing layers. None of them is a layer of its own.

- **1 Fear.** Position 1, on “I am not the kind of person who”. Position 10, when the
  ladder bottoms out at “I am a fraud”, the commonest rung-4 belief in the instrument.
- **2 Inheritance.** Position 2, operational form only: which defaults have since been
  taken up on purpose. Position 7, on which inherited background still does real work.
- **3 Role.** Position 1, on “it would be immodest”, once the ladder has run on it. The
  category is a description she accepted rather than a property she has.
- **4 Voices.** Position 7 is the corrective that keeps the layer out of nihilism.
  Subtracting borrowed wants leaves nothing at all unless some background survives the
  subtraction. Ask which one does.
- **5 Field.** Position 6 with all three anti-fiction safeguards, run over her list.
  Position 12, on what her recurring vocabulary keeps looking at.
- **6 Standing.** Position 10, on the identity being defended. Position 8, on the
  reception that was never hers to control.
- **7 Poisons.** Position 8 is the main instrument for envy and for despair about
  reception. Position 3, on resentment about timing.
- **8 Contact.** Position 12 directly. Position 9 on forcing: the state she describes
  when work goes well is the unforced one, and naming the friction finds the destroyer.
- **9 Epoch.** Nothing from this file. Layer 9 has its own four positions in
  `references/epoch.md`, and mixing the two sets produces mush.
- **10 Resource and 11 Stakes.** Written separately; this file assumes nothing about
  their content. Whatever they hold, the fits are position 8 for capacity, money and
  reception, position 9 for forcing and depletion, positions 4 and 5 for what the work
  has to have done by the end, and position 3 for pricing the time already spent.
- **Ground, the crisis mode.** Position 4 first and alone. Position 5 second, once she
  is talking again. Position 9 third, for “nobody needs it”. Nothing else. Never run
  position 1 in Ground: telling someone in collapse that she is choosing it is the
  worst move available.
